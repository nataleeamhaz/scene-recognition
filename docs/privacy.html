Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-12

Room Design Recommender is an individual hobby project, not a commercial product. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What data is collected

  • Room photos you upload for analysis
  • Optional text describing your design goal

How it's used

  • Uploaded photos are analyzed for furniture detection and style/design recommendations
  • Photos and prompts may be sent to third-party AI providers (e.g. Anthropic) to generate recommendations
  • Style/mood keywords derived from your photo are used to search Pinterest for design inspiration via the Pinterest API

What is not done

  • Photos are not sold or shared with advertisers
  • Photos are not permanently stored by this app beyond the request needed to process them

Pinterest data

This app uses the Pinterest API to search for design inspiration pins. It does not post, modify, or delete anything on your Pinterest account. Access can be revoked at any time from your Pinterest account settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via the project's GitHub repository.
